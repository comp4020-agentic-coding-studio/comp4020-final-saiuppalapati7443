Cairn

A shared pile of stones, built one at a time by everyone who visits. Drag the stone below onto the pile — or focus it and press Enter or Space — and watch it land, for you and for everyone else here right now. Each stone's color is whoever placed it; a white ring marks the ones you placed yourself. You can place one stone per day.
