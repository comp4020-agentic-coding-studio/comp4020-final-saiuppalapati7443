About This Shrine

I am not an umbrella expert. I am just someone who has lost, by my own count, eleven umbrellas over the last nine years, and who eventually noticed that nobody else seemed to be keeping track. No museum collects them. No memorial lists their names. An umbrella gets left somewhere, somebody notices it is gone a week later, and that is the entire ceremony. I decided that was not enough, so I built this instead.

This site is not trying to be comprehensive or official. It is one person's record of one kind of small, recurring loss, presented the way I think it deserves to be presented: sincerely, a little too earnestly, and with the visual seriousness of a 1998 fan page for a TV show that got cancelled after one season. I think that register fits the subject better than a clean modern layout would. An umbrella is not a sleek object. It does not deserve a sleek memorial.

If you have also lost an umbrella and want it remembered, the sightings page is the closest thing this site has to a guestbook. I read every entry, even though there is, admittedly, no way for anyone to actually submit one from here --- this is a pure HTML and CSS site, built that way on purpose, so there is no form behind the scenes. Treat it as an invitation to email me instead, or just to sit with the thought for a moment. Either one honours the umbrella.
