Sightings

This is my own log, kept the way a guestbook might be kept if only one person ever signed it. Whenever I notice an umbrella that someone else has abandoned --- on a platform, in a gutter, wedged behind a bus stop --- I write it down here. These are not my umbrellas; they belong (or belonged) to strangers. This page is just where I keep track of them.

  • Platform 2, train station. Red, fully closed, good condition. Leaning against a bench like it was waiting for its owner, not lost. I watched it through an entire twenty-minute delay and nobody came back for it.
  • Hedge outside a house on my street. Striped, open, slightly bent spokes. Must have blown out of someone's hand on a windy evening and ended up here. I left it; it looked more at home in the hedge than it would in my hands.
  • Train station rubbish bin. Black, inside out, spokes snapped. The single most common kind of sighting I log. I almost stopped noting these, then decided they deserved the record more than the dramatic ones do.
  • Cafe table, outdoor seating. Clear plastic dome, closed, undamaged. Sat untouched through an entire lunch service while three different parties used the chair next to it. Staff eventually moved it inside; I assume it is still there.
  • Propped against a lecture hall door. Oversized golf umbrella, navy, closed, barely used. Too large and too intact to be an accident --- someone decided to leave this one behind. I noted it and kept walking.
