# Crit 9 reflection

1. What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

    The breakthrough was deciding that carrying a stone stays local and only
    the landed stone is shared. At first I assumed a real-time app should
    send everything live, but when I checked that against my README, the
    point of the cairn is the trace someone chooses to leave, not watching
    them hesitate while they decide. Writing the decision record and the
    strongest argument my pod could make against it showed me what the
    choice costs (less live co-presence), and turning it into a CLAUDE.md
    rule and a spec test meant it became something the app actually
    enforces, not just something I said.

2. What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

    This week changed how I think about my role. The agent wrote most of the
    code, but deciding what the app should not do, and being able to defend
    that to my pod, was the part that needed me. I want to be the developer
    who can explain why a feature is missing, not just add every feature
    because it's possible. Next week I want to understand failures myself
    before handing them to the agent.
